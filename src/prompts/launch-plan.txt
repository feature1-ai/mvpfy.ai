  ],
  "secrets": [

      "fromYou": true | false }
  ],
  "steps": ["<what would happen, in order, one short plain-language line each>"],
  "notes": ["<anything they should know before agreeing — limits, first-month charges, manual steps>"]
}

Rules:

• Every service in the compose file is either in "resources", or in "notes" with a sentence
  saying why it is not needed in production. Do not silently drop one — a missing queue or
  cache is a product that breaks a week after launch.
• "monthlyUsd" is per resource, and must be a real number from that provider's public
  pricing, for the size you named. Do not guess wildly, do not write 0 for something that
  costs money, and do not include a free trial credit — mvpfy adds these up and shows the
  builder the total before they agree to anything.
• "fromYou" is true for any secret only they can supply — a live payment key, a real API
  token, an SMTP account. False for values that can be generated at deploy time.
• "steps" are what the builder would watch happen, in their language. Not CLI commands.
• Never include a secret VALUE anywhere in this file, only the names.
• Do not claim the product is ready to launch, and do not restate the readiness findings.
  Whether it launches at all is decided elsewhere.
