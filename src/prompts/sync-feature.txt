Hard rules:
• Never create_feature, and never delete anything in Feature1. A story removed from the
  plan here is left alone there and named in your summary — deleting someone's work
  because a plan was edited is not a sync.
• Do not call generate_prompts_for_all_acs: that starts work, and this run is bookkeeping.
• Never move a story further along than its lane here says. This board is the truth being
  copied, not a starting point.
• Do not modify any other file, do not implement anything, do not run docker or git.
• If a step fails, say which and why, and carry on with the rest: a Feature1 that is right
  about the PRD and four stories out of five beats one that stopped at the first error.
